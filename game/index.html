Sizzle & Serve

A tiny kitchen rush game. Cook, chop, stack and serve before the orders run out.

WASD / arrows move E / Space pick up, chop, cook, stack, serve Mouse click a counter to walk there, click again to use it R restart after game over

Recipes: bun, patty, cheese, tomato, lettuce — in the order shown on the plate station. Raw patties cook on the stove, tomatoes and lettuce need the cutting board. Serve finished plates at the window. Three expired orders ends the shift.
